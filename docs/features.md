# How to play

You are a snake floating inside a see-through sphere among the stars. You always move forward; you only choose where to turn.

## The goal

Eat as many glowing yellow dots as you can. Four dots float in the sphere at a time, and each one you eat scores a point and is replaced by a new one somewhere else. Your score shows in the top right corner while you play.

## Growing

You start four segments long, and every dot adds three more to your tail.

## Dots come to you

When your head passes close to a dot, the dot slides toward it, faster the closer it gets. You don't have to hit a dot exactly to eat it.

## What ends a run

- Running your head into your own body.
- Touching the wall of the sphere.

The **Game Over** screen shows your score and your best score so far. Choose **Play Again** to start a new run, or **Main Menu** to go back.

## Pausing

Press `Esc` (or `Start` on a gamepad) to pause. The pause menu has **Resume** and **Quit to Menu**.

## Tips

- Plan your turns ahead: three new segments per dot add up quickly.
- Keep away from the wall. It's as deadly as your own body.
